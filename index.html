Who we are

About our club

The Rotaract Club of Yerevan brings together young people aged roughly 18–30 who want to grow as leaders while giving back to our city. We are proudly sponsored by a local Rotary club, and our members organize and take part in service projects, professional development events, and fellowship activities throughout the year.

🌱

Founded

Our club was founded in Yerevan and has been active ever since, growing a little more with every new member and project.

🤝

What we do

From community clean-ups to professional workshops, we run hands-on projects across Yerevan, built and led entirely by our members.

🎯

Our mission

To develop young leaders who create positive, lasting change in their communities through service, fellowship, and international understanding.

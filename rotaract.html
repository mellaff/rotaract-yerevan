What is Rotaract?

A global network of young people who turn ideas into action.

The basics

A global youth service organization

Rotaract is a global service organization that brings together young people aged roughly 18 to 30 who share a passion for community service, professional growth, and international friendship. Rotaract clubs exist in countries all over the world, each one designing and running its own service projects while staying connected to a much larger global network.


Every Rotaract club is sponsored by a local Rotary club — a partnership that gives Rotaractors access to mentorship, resources, and a worldwide community of experienced professionals, while Rotaractors bring fresh energy, new ideas, and youthful perspective to the causes Rotary champions.

Our values

What Rotaract stands for

Four ideas guide everything a Rotaract club does, from a small local clean-up to an international convention.

🛠️

Service

Taking real, hands-on action to address needs in our community and beyond.

🧭

Leadership

Developing the confidence and skills to lead projects, teams, and change.

🎉

Fellowship

Building genuine friendships with people who share our drive to give back.

🤝

International Understanding

Connecting with Rotaractors worldwide to learn from different cultures and ideas.

⚙️

Rotary connection

Sponsored by Rotary International

Rotaract is one of several programs supported by Rotary International, the global organization best known for its work eradicating polio and funding humanitarian projects worldwide. Each Rotaract club partners with a local Rotary club, which acts as a sponsor and mentor — attending events, offering guidance, and helping open doors — while Rotaract members remain independent in choosing and running their own projects.
